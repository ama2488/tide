privacy policy · effective [DATE]

persi collects nothing. everything you enter stays on your phone.

what persi stores

Your cycle settings, the periods you log, the rituals you add and the actions you check off are saved only on your device, inside the app. They are never sent to us or to anyone else. persi has no accounts, no servers, no analytics and no advertising.

apple health

If you choose to connect Apple Health, persi reads the menstrual flow days stored there to estimate where you are in your cycle. persi never writes to Apple Health. Health data is used only inside the app, on your device. It is never shared, sold, or used for advertising or marketing, and it is never stored in iCloud.

You can turn off persi's access at any time in Settings → Health → Data Access & Devices → persi.

tracking

persi does not track you across apps or websites, and contains no third-party code that does.

deleting your data

Use "start over" in persi's settings, or delete the app. Both remove everything persi has stored. If you back up your iPhone, your persi data is included in that backup, which Apple protects under your own account.

children

persi does not knowingly collect any information from anyone, including children.

not medical advice

persi offers general wellness ideas. It is not a medical device, a contraceptive or a fertility tool. Talk with a clinician before starting any new herb or supplement.
